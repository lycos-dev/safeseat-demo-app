SafeSeat R71 — Android Sticky Tabs + iOS Slider Gesture Fix
PATCH ONLY — apply over R70.

Updated files:
- src/app/(tabs)/settings/index.tsx
- src/components/escalation-window-control.tsx

Fixes:
1. Settings section shortcuts no longer rely on React Native's native sticky-header touch handling.
   A top overlay is shown only after the original shortcut bar reaches the top.
   The overlay sits outside the ScrollView, uses Android elevation/z-index, and receives taps directly.

2. Programmatic Account / Alerts / History / System jumps keep the chosen green tab stable while the scroll position updates, preventing iOS selection flicker.

3. While Driver SMS Delay is being dragged, the Settings ScrollView is temporarily disabled.
   This prevents the iOS page from moving vertically while the slider is being manipulated.
   Scrolling is restored as soon as the finger is released or the gesture is cancelled.
