# SafeSeat UAT UX R30.1 — Session History Visibility Fix

- Moved **Session History** from the bottom of the long System section into the **Account** group near the top of Settings.
- The Session History route remains registered at `/(tabs)/settings/session-history`.
- Session History is always visible whether there are zero, one, or many completed sessions.
- The empty-history screen remains available when no sessions have been completed yet.
- No changes were made to the per-seat session data model or privacy boundary.
